Start by opening an ingestion request using our [submission guide]({{< relref "/info/contrib.md" >}}).
Your answers will be used to add a personalized checklist and timeline to the issue; check off tasks as you complete them.
Colocated workshop chairs should coordinate through their main conference publication chairs instead.
