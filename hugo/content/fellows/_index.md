The ACL Fellows program recognizes members whose work has made extraordinary
contributions to computational linguistics through research, service,
education, or outreach. The program began in 2011. The ACL Lifetime Achievement
Award recognizes sustained, foundational contributions to the field.

Induction years follow the [official ACL Fellows roster](https://www.aclweb.org/aclwiki/ACL_Fellows).
Lifetime Achievement Award years and acceptance speeches follow the
[official recipients list](https://www.aclweb.org/adminwiki/index.php/ACL_Lifetime_Achievement_Award_Recipients).
