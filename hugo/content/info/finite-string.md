---
title: The Finite String newsletter
---

_The Finite String_ was the newsletter of the Association for Computational Linguistics. Its publication format changed over time: it began as a paper newsletter, was distributed on microfiche with the _American Journal of Computational Linguistics_ (AJCL), appeared as a section within AJCL and _Computational Linguistics_ (CL), and later returned to paper supplements.

## Anthology coverage

The [Finite String venue](/venues/finitestring/) contains one collection per publication year and one volume per physical newsletter issue. There are 145 issue volumes in 27 yearly collections:

- **55 digitized issues:** 30 complete microfiche-era issues from 1974-1979 and 25 newsletter sections assembled from AJCL/CL scans from 1980-1987.
- **90 catalog-only issues:** 84 dated entries from 1964-1973 and six later paper supplements from 1988, 1991, and 1992. These have issue metadata and landing pages but await usable scans.

Combined numbers and split parts retain their printed issue labels. For example, [1964 volume 1, number 6, part 1](/volumes/1964.finitestring-6a/) and [part 2](/volumes/1964.finitestring-6b/) are separate physical issues, while [1972 numbers 1-2](/volumes/1972.finitestring-1to2/) are one combined issue.

Volume 15, number 5 was distributed with the delayed final AJCL issue for 1978, but its scan says that it was released on March 24, 1979 and is copyrighted 1979. It is therefore cataloged as [1979.finitestring-5](/volumes/1979.finitestring-5/).

[J75-3004](/J75-3004/), cited as potentially erroneous in [issue #1695](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/1695), is a genuine continuation page. The scan is page 92 of microfiche 22 and contains the newsletter's "Personal Notes" section.

## Catalog-only holdings

The [archived Finite String index](https://web.archive.org/web/20211201201347/https://www.aclweb.org/old_anthology/docs/fs.html) lists the following issues without scans:

| 1973 | Volume 10, numbers 7-8 | One combined issue is listed; numbers 1-6 are not listed. |

| 1991 | Volume 17, numbers 1-3 | May, June, and September 1991 paper supplements. |
| 1992 | Volume 18, numbers 1-2 | March and June 1992 paper supplements. |

These 90 entries now have canonical volume pages. The 84 entries from 1964-1973 count combined numbers as one physical issue and the two parts of volume 1, number 6 separately. [Issue #6008](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/6008) reports 86 physical pre-1974 issues, so the two inventories still need to be reconciled.

[Issue #1695](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/1695) reports scans from 1992 and 1993 that have not yet been deposited in the Anthology. The archived index has no 1993 entry. No Finite String supplement was found in the digitized `J88` through `J93` journal volumes.

## Sources and remaining work

This inventory reconciles the local metadata and scans with the archived index and [issues #1695](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/1695), [#5606](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/5606), and [#6008](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/6008). Completing the venue requires obtaining usable scans for the 90 catalog-only issues, confirming the two-entry discrepancy for 1964-1973, and determining whether any issues existed in the gaps not represented by the archived index.
