---
title: The Finite String newsletter
---

_The Finite String_ was the newsletter of the Association for Computational Linguistics. Its publication format changed over time: it began as a paper newsletter, was distributed on microfiche with the _American Journal of Computational Linguistics_ (AJCL), appeared as a section within AJCL and _Computational Linguistics_ (CL), and later returned to paper supplements.

## How this venue is represented

The Anthology currently associates venues with volumes, not individual papers. The [Finite String venue](/venues/finitestring/) therefore lists the 44 AJCL/CL volumes that contain identified newsletter material. Those are mixed volumes and also contain ordinary journal articles. The table below gives the exact 157 Anthology records whose scans or metadata identify them as _Finite String_ material.

For the 1974-1979 microfiche run, some newsletter issues were split into several Anthology records. Research papers extracted from those issues remain cataloged as AJCL papers, following the reorganization in [PR #285](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/pull/285), and can be found on the linked containing-volume pages.

| 1991 | Volume 17, numbers 1-3 | May, June, and September 1991 paper supplements. |
| 1992 | Volume 18, numbers 1-2 | March and June 1992 paper supplements. |

This is 84 dated entries for 1964-1973 when combined numbers count as one entry and the two parts of volume 1, number 6 count separately, plus six later supplements. [Issue #6008](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/6008) reports 86 physical pre-1974 issues, so the two inventories still need to be reconciled.

[Issue #1695](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/1695) reports scans from 1992 and 1993 that have not yet been deposited in the Anthology. The archived index has no 1993 entry. No Finite String supplement was found in the digitized `J88` through `J93` journal volumes.

## Sources and remaining work

This inventory reconciles the local metadata and scans with the archived index and [issues #1695](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/1695), [#5606](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/5606), and [#6008](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/6008). Completing the venue requires obtaining usable scans for the catalog-only issues, confirming the two-entry discrepancy for 1964-1973, and determining whether any issues existed in the gaps not represented by the archived index.
