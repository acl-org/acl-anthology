# EACL 2026 award evidence

The original awards were added for [issue #10137](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/10137).
Each paper was matched by title to its Anthology ID using the `acl_anthology`
library. Workshop awards are attributed to their respective workshops, not to
the main EACL conference.

Additional conference awards and the award explanations come from the supplied
"EACL 2026 Paper Awards" document. Its paper numbers are submission numbers,
not Anthology IDs.
