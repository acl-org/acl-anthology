# EACL 2026 award evidence

Awards added for [issue #10137](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology/issues/10137).
Each paper was matched by title to its Anthology ID using the `acl_anthology`
library. Workshop awards are attributed to their respective workshops, not to
the main EACL conference.

| Paper ID | Award annotation | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `2026.eacl-long.32` | Best Paper Award | [Stanford Linguistics](https://linguistics.stanford.edu/news/stanford-linguists-eacl-2026) names "Humans and transformer LMs: Abstraction drives language learning" as the EACL 2026 Best Paper. |
| `2026.eacl-srw.40` | SRW Best Paper Award | [Ehime University](https://mcsp.gsse.ehime-u.ac.jp/news/E8L8_1sq) announces an EACL 2026 SRW Best Paper Award for Shoto Nishida's "Probabilistic Bilingual Subword Segmentation with Latent Subword Alignment". |
| `2026.mme-main.6` | Best Paper Award | The [MME workshop](https://multilingual-multicultural-evaluation.github.io/) names "The Anthropology of Food: How NLP can Help us Unravel the Food cultures of the World" as its Best Paper Award winner. |
| `2026.mme-main.4` | Best Paper Honourable Mention | The [MME workshop](https://multilingual-multicultural-evaluation.github.io/) names "Vinclat: Evaluating Reasoning, Cognition and Culture in One Game" as its Best Paper Honourable Mention. |
| `2026.teachingnlp-1.14` | Best Paper Award | [Fondazione Bruno Kessler](https://mt.fbk.eu/best-paper-award-at-teachnlp-2026/) identifies "Practising responsibility: Ethics in NLP as a hands-on course" as the TeachNLP 2026 Best Paper at EACL. |

The [EACL conference awards page](https://2026.eacl.org/program/best-paper/)
does not currently name winners. Other purported EACL 2026 award recipients
were not annotated without a source naming both the paper and the award.
